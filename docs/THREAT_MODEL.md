# Data and authority boundaries

## Present tooling

Maintained source, selected executable files and the locked tooling environment are inputs to repository controls. Received descriptors and inventories cannot select policy or acquire execution authority. Checks inspect bounded local scopes and report fixed diagnostics. Byte identity differs from interpretation and qualification.

An inventory is not a concurrent filesystem snapshot or power-failure durability guarantee. Native children use finite argument vectors and direct-child deadlines; completed-output limits are not live volume ceilings. Foundation qualification is in progress.

## Future mutation sources

Campaigns, protocols, patches, attempts and reports are supplied data. They do not grant shell execution, signing, equipment control, provider access, spending or publication authority. Registry references cannot select arbitrary executable text. Actual adapters and source admission precede use.

Preserve complete source, suite and generated identities, baseline outcomes, every actual attempt and all unresolved work. No silent favourable subset, changed evaluated suite, omitted mutant, fabricated kill or guessed equivalence is admissible.

Unknown source law, numerical method or current trust cannot become positive eligibility. Shared evidence, equipment and operational recovery remain separate unimplemented boundaries; no isolation, scientific or durability guarantee is claimed here.
